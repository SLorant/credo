project-subheadline-1: "FIGHTING FOR AN ONLINE PRESENCE: FROM THE GYM TO THE DIGITAL MARKET"
project-text-1: "The operations of the Szeged-based New Generation Fight Team
  are built on combat sports instruction and functional performance enhancement.
  While the club’s service model operates exceptionally well on a local level,
  the capacity constraints of in-person training hindered further business
  growth. The goal of our collaboration was to extract the methodological
  expertise and training theory framework of the gym's head coach from the
  physical space and transform it into a high-value digital product that can be
  sold without geographical limitations. The task was not simply to create a
  basic training plan, but rather to execute a strategically positioned digital
  product development with unique visual value capable of capturing meaningful
  market share in the online sector. Our agency was responsible for the entire
  product concept development: from the structural blueprint and
  audience-specific copywriting strategy to the design of the dedicated sales
  landing page and digital add-ons."

project-subheadline-2: METHODOLOGICAL STRATEGY AND CONVERSION-OPTIMIZED PRODUCT DESIGN
project-text-2: >-
  The backbone of the project was provided by the club's head coach's
  professional material, which we reshaped into a structured and easily
  digestible digital product. In the first phase, we developed the entire
  copywriting structure: enriching the sports science theoretical background
  with unique motivational blocks and branding elements showcasing the club's
  philosophy. To enhance user experience and efficiency, we assigned embedded
  video guides to the exercises, directly supporting the comprehension of the
  methodology.


  In parallel with the visual design of the nearly 70-page, brand-consistent
  digital training plan and interactive supplementary annexes in Figma, we
  placed a major focus on the product's dedicated sales landing page. We fully
  provided the conversion-optimized copywriting, UX/UI design, and front-end
  development for this brand-new subpage integrated into the club's website.
  During the design process, we applied a strict visual hierarchy: equipping the
  page with strategically placed CTA elements and marketing-relevant messaging
  tailored to the relevant target audiences, ensuring high engagement and a
  logically structured sales funnel to minimize cart abandonment.
